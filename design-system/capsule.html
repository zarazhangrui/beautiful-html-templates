01 — Overview

A magazine that took a holiday
at an ice-cream parlor

Capsule is a playful editorial system whose defining premise is the pill: every text container, every icon, every bar, every node is a pill with a 2px ink stroke. Confident Bodoni serif headlines, a nine-color candy palette, and floating decorative pills as atmospheric wallpaper.

02 — Colours

Nine Candies, Three Neutrals

03 — Typography

A Two-Face Conversation

Glamorous didone serif statements + clean grotesk supporting structure. Bodoni headlines are sentence-case and always ink; Space Grotesk small text is always uppercase with 0.08em+ tracking.

05 — Layout

Populated, Never Bare

Slides feel populated: one or two content blocks paired with 5–8 floating decorative pills and 1–3 radial glows. Empty corners read as broken — the candy palette wants to participate.

06 — Depth & Shape

Soft Offset, Never Blur

The only depth technique is a solid hard-offset shadow in low-opacity ink at 8% — it reads as a soft lift, not a stamp. Always bottom-right. The 2px outline does the rest. Decorative pills stay flat.

07 — Do & Don't

Keep It Candy, Keep It Sharp

✓ Do
  • Make every text container a pill — 9999px small, 2rem cards — with the 2px ink outline.
  • Set every Bodoni headline in ink; put colour on stat numerals and pill fills.
  • Render shadows as solid offsets at 4/6/8/12px in the 8%-ink shadow colour.
  • Populate backgrounds with 5–8 tilted decorative pills and radial glows.
  • Wrap inline emphasis in a candy quote-highlight pill, not bold.
✕ Don't
  • Render any sharp-cornered text container, or any pill without its 2px stroke.
  • Colour a Bodoni headline, or uppercase it — serifs are sentence case.
  • Use blurred drop shadows, or re-colour the offset shadow.
  • Pair Bodoni with a different sans, or add a tenth accent colour.
  • Leave a declarative slide's corners empty — bare cream reads as broken.

08 — 中文 / CJK

Candy In Chinese

Strategy A — a single font-stack with CJK fallback after the Latin face. 站酷小薇体 ZCOOL XiaoWei echoes Bodoni's didone modulation for headlines; 悠哉字体 Yozai mirrors Space Grotesk for body. Both sit cleanly inside candy pills with the ink outline.

胶囊设计系统
每一个容器都是一颗药丸：圆角、墨黑描边、柔和的偏移投影。站酷小薇体承载标题的高反差气质，悠哉字体处理正文与药丸文字。装饰药丸换成单字氛围词（愿景、未来、下一步），糖果配色照常运转。盘古之白：在中文与 Latin 之间留一个空格，如 AI 产品、2026 年。
