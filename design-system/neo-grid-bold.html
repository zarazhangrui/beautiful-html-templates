03 — Typography

Heavy Caps + Mono

Space Grotesk 700 uppercase with negative tracking carries every display moment; JetBrains Mono uppercase with wide tracking carries every label. Body is Space Grotesk 400 mixed case. No third face. Emphasis is the yellow swatch or an em colour-switch — never italic.

05 — Layout

12 Columns, 8 Rows

The universal frame is a 12-column × 8-row CSS grid, inset 40px from the slide edge, 12px gaps. Composition is pure grid-column / grid-row spans — flexbox is used only inside a single cell. Fill the grid corner to corner; sparse grids read as broken.

06 — Depth & Shape

Adjacency, Not Shadow

Zero box-shadows. Zero rounded corners. Zero gradients (except photo-region noise). Depth is entirely a function of which colour sits next to which — a paper panel beside ink reads as forward; a lemon panel beside ink is the loudest signal. The only borders are 1.5px ink on tables & pills.

07 — Do & Don't

Hold The Grid

Do
  • Compose every slide on the 12×8 grid; fill it corner to corner.
  • Use paper as default, ink for contrast, lemon for 1–3 signal panels.
  • Set display in Space Grotesk 700 uppercase with negative tracking.
  • Use <mark> for a yellow swatch or <em> for a yellow colour-switch.
  • Label everything in JetBrains Mono uppercase with 0.08em+ tracking.
Don't
  • Add box-shadow, round a corner, or use a gradient on a panel.
  • Introduce a second accent colour, or use lemon as text.
  • Put display in mixed case, or body in uppercase.
  • Use italic letterforms — <em> is a colour switch, not a slant.
  • Sparsely populate the grid — density is the editorial identity.
08 — 中文 / CJK

Weight, Not Caps

Chinese has no uppercase, so the brutalist signature transfers through weight: set every display moment in 思源黑体 Noto Sans SC 900 to match Space Grotesk 700 ALL CAPS, and drop the negative tracking entirely (CJK glyphs collide when tightened). The yellow <mark> swatch transfers perfectly.
