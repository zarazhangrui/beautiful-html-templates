01 — OverviewVellum / Essay-on-a-wall
The Premise

A Monochromatic Essay, Pinned

Vellum is severe and tender at once: every slide is the same deep periwinkle field, with warm chartreuse-yellow italic Cormorant floating centered on it. No alternate surface, no light theme, no second background. The field is the constant; the type and one small teal annotation in the corner are everything else.

03 — TypographyItalic serif · sans · typed mono
Three Faces

Italic Is The Default

Cormorant Garamond · italic 400 · personality
Considered & intimate
An old-style serif with an expressive italic axis. Carries every headline, numeral, and quote — in italic at weight 400, never roman. Italic at display scale against the periwinkle field is the system's defining moment.
DM Sans · 400 · the quiet substance
Body recedes behind the serif.
A clean humanist grotesque for body and lead paragraphs. It is the substance, not the voice — meant to recede so the italic headlines lead the eye.
Courier Prime · the typed-note voice

05 — LayoutCentered · sparse
Pinned & Centered

Centered, With Air

Content rarely fills more than 70% of width or 60% of height — the remaining navy is the pinned-wall context. Every layout centers; the pin-annotation in the bottom-left corner is the one element present on every slide.

06 — Depth & ShapeCompletely flat · still
No Elevation, No Motion

Completely Flat

07 — Do & Don'tHold the register
Hold The Register

Hold The Register

Do
  1. Fill every slide with the single navy field — there is no light alternate.
  2. Render every headline in italic Cormorant 400, in yellow.
  3. Place a teal pin-annotation in the bottom-left corner of every slide.
  4. Use <em> as upright roman 600 in emphasis-yellow.
  5. Number lists with Courier counters in teal; center all content.
Don't
  1. Introduce a second background colour or a light theme.
  2. Render headlines in upright roman, except as <em>.
  3. Add drop shadows, rounded corners, gradients, or motion.
  4. Use yellow on the quote-mark or as a kicker colour.
  5. Use bullet dots or em-dashes, or crowd the field edge-to-edge.

08 — 中文 / CJKStrategy C · literary stack
Handwritten Chartreuse

A Kaiti On The Wall

Latin stays in Cormorant italic; Chinese drops into 霞鹜文楷 LXGW WenKai — the kaiti's hand-written warmth mirrors Cormorant's intimate italic voice. Against the periwinkle field, the chartreuse kaiti reads as handwritten chartreuse on a gallery wall.

羊皮卷上的随笔
单一的长春花蓝场，居中漂浮的暖黄字，角落里一张打字机便签。霞鹜文楷承载标题的个人语气，思源宋体安静地处理正文。中文引号改用「」，与 teal 引号字形呼应。盘古之白：在中文与 Latin 之间留一个空格，如 使用 Claude、2026 年。
