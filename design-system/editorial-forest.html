Overview§ 01 / Editorial Forest

One serif voice,
used with conviction.

The foundational premise is a single, confident typographic voice — Source Serif 4 — used at extreme scale for headlines and stat figures, with JetBrains Mono playing the supporting role of editorial chrome. The serif runs at weight 500 for nearly every display moment: heavy enough to feel authored, light enough to keep the page calm.

Register
Penguin classic, quiet annual report, art-book spread
Surfaces
Forest green · dusty pink · oat cream paper
Depth model
Flat — colour-block contrast and 2px rules only
A
Two faces, never three

Source Serif 4 carries every editorial moment. JetBrains Mono carries every label, caption, axis tick, and footline. The system never mixes a third typeface.

B
Weight 500 display

The serif runs at 500 — never the familiar 400 or 700. Optical-size axis engaged, so the same face renders with size-aware letterforms from 26px caption to 220px display.

C
Spacious & committed

One strong subject per slide, surrounded by deep negative space. Padding runs 96–160px. Fewer elements at larger sizes, never more elements at smaller ones.

D
Printed, not glassy

No drop shadows, no glows, no gradients. Elevation is communicated entirely through colour-block contrast and the difference between a filled region and a bordered one.

Colours§ 02 / Tri-tone

Three surfaces,
two near-duplicates.

A tight five-colour editorial set built around three primary surfaces. Two surface tones per slide is typical; three is loud. Introducing a fourth chromatic family breaks the editorial discipline — the green / pink / cream triad does all the work.

Typography§ 03 / Serif + Mono

Source Serif 4 at 500.

The weight 500 + optical-size + tight-tracking combination is the system's display voice. Switch any of those three — weight 700, default tracking, no opsz — and it reads as a different design system. The role split is strict: mono never appears at headline scale, serif never at axis-tick scale.

Labels · Captions · Axis Ticks · Footlines

Every mono element is uppercase with at least 0.08em letter-spacing. The wide tracking is what makes mono feel like a caption row in a magazine, not a terminal. The roles are inverted from the usual stack — mono for chrome, serif for body and display. Italics and underline are never used; emphasis is size and colour.

Layout§ 05 / 1920 × 1080

The topbar is the spine.

A fixed 1920×1080 canvas, scaled to fit by the deck runtime. Every slide carries a topbar — a mono label on one edge, a monogram or counter on the other. Heavier slides also carry a footline. Without the topbar, a slide reads as untreated.

Depth & Shape§ 06 / Flat

The absence of shadow
is the elevation language.

There are no shadows in this system — not on cards, not on tiles, not on text, not on the monogram circle. Adding a box-shadow would shatter the paper feel. Elevation lives in three places only.

01
Colour-block contrast

A green tile on a cream slide reads as elevated because the dark block separates from the paper ground.

2px hairline rules

Section separators sit on a 2px line in the region's accent. Never 1px (reads as web app), never 3px+ (reads as poster).

Do & Don't§ 07 / Discipline

Keep the triad tight.

Do

  • +Run every headline in Source Serif 4 at 500 with negative tracking and tight line-height.
  • +Run every label, tag, and footline in JetBrains Mono 500 uppercase, 0.08–0.18em.
  • +Place a topbar on every slide — it is the system's spine.
  • +Pick one dominant surface per slide; two colours is the maximum.
  • +Rotate tile fills: green + pink + green-lite + cream-2-with-border.
  • +Scale display type to 140–220px for statement, hero, and closing moments.

Don't

  • –Don't add box-shadow to any element — it shatters the paper feel.
  • –Don't introduce a third typeface, or a fourth chromatic family.
  • –Don't run serif in italic or underline — emphasis is size and colour.
  • –Don't render mono in sentence case or without letter-spacing.
  • –Don't run body text at weight 500 — it reads heavy. Body is 400.
  • –Don't pile two competing content blocks on one slide.

中文 / CJK§ 08 / Strategy C

安静的
编辑气质

保持 Source Serif 4 作为拉丁字面,中文按字形回退到霞鹜文楷(标题、展示)或思源宋体(正文、数字)。霞鹜文楷与 500 字重的衬线同样有手作般的温度,正是这套系统「企鹅经典」气质的中文对应。

Universal CJK adjustments

  • 放宽行高 0.05–0.08:中文是全宽方块,比拉丁字母视觉更重 —— 展示提到 1.0–1.1,正文提到 1.5–1.6。
  • 去掉中文标题的负字距:衬线展示用的 -0.03em 会让汉字相互挤压,中文设为 letter-spacing 0。
  • 使用中文全角标点(,。:;「」),不与拉丁标点混用;标题结尾不加句号。
  • 在中英文边界加盘古之白:「2026 年 5 月」而非「2026年5月」。
  • chrome 标签仍用等宽体 —— 不要让中文标签退回文楷或宋体,否则会失去打字机般的编辑质感。
