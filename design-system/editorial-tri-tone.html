§ 01 — Overview

Three colours,
told in full.

Eleven CSS variable names. Only three hex values exist.

The closed system

Every alias is a semantic rename of one of three colours. --pink and --sky point at the same blush; --cream, --butter, --lime, --terracotta all resolve to one golden yellow; --burgundy, --navy, --forest, --ink all collapse to one deep wine.

The aliases exist to communicate a colour's role in context — never to introduce variation.

A three-way conversation

Bricolage Grotesque carries display and body across weights 400–800. Instrument Serif appears only for moments of human expressiveness — numerals, years, signatures, quote marks. JetBrains Mono carries every label and section marker.

The voice is an independent arts publication: a colophon, hand-numbered editions, an editorial desk.

§ 07 — Do & Don't

Hold the three.

Do

  • №Treat the three hex values as a closed system — every element is pink, butter, or burgundy.
  • №Use Instrument Serif only for numerals, years, signatures, and quote marks.
  • №Use the em rule to trigger the serif switch in grotesque headlines.
  • №Give every mono label 0.10–0.18em positive tracking.
  • №Open every slide with the § NN — Title marker convention.

Don't

  • №Don't introduce a fourth colour — no neutrals, grays, or white.
  • №Don't use Instrument Serif at body sizes or for running text.
  • №Don't put solid dividers on burgundy — use low-opacity rgba.
  • №Don't apply box-shadow. Depth is surface contrast and radius.
  • №Don't use weight 800 except for the single wordmark.

The em-rule does not port to Chinese

  • 行内换脸在中文里读作排版错误 —— 拉丁标题用 <em> 切到 Instrument Serif 斜体,中文则改用颜色或字重强调,绝不在句中换字体。
  • 放宽行高 0.05–0.08:全宽方块比拉丁字母更重,展示提到 0.95–1.05,正文提到 1.5–1.6。
  • 去掉中文标题负字距(Bricolage 展示用 -0.04 至 -0.06em 会挤压汉字),设为 letter-spacing 0。
  • 使用中文全角标点(,。:;「」),标题结尾不加句号;中英文边界加盘古之白:「2024 年春」。
  • 区段标记 § NN — 标题 在中文同样成立:符号与数字保持等宽,标题切思源黑体 400 配 0.05em 字距。
