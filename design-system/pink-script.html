01 — Overview

A Late-Night Lookbook

Pink Script is a nocturnal couture editorial system built on a single atmospheric premise: a deep warm-black surface lit from the upper-left, a film-grain overlay, and a hairline interior frame. Over it, oversized DM Serif Display in hot fuchsia anchors every slide. Closer to a Maison's seasonal lookbook than a startup deck.

02 — Colours

Monochromatically Pink

One signature: hot fuchsia against deep warm-black, with pearl paper-blush as the editorial ink. The pink is the entire accent system — script colour, kicker, rule, pill, inline emphasis, and the soft halo behind hero scripts. No second chromatic accent.

03 — Typography

Three Editorial Voices

DM Serif Display carries every script & editorial moment from 32px to 600px (script = pink, headline = paper). Inter 300 is the calm prose voice. JetBrains Mono is the boutique-catalog metadata. Italic is never used — <em> is repurposed as a pink colour switch.

05 — Layout

One Hero Moment

A single oversized script anchors the slide, with the rest as generous negative space punctuated by a runner, a kicker, a paragraph, and a footer. The hero takes 60–70% of the canvas; supporting copy orbits the margin. Cramming equal-weight regions breaks the late-night feeling.

06 — Depth & Shape

Atmospheric, Not Structural

Zero box-shadows. Depth comes from three layers: the off-center radial surface (a studio softbox catching one corner), the film-grain overlay, and the pink halo text-shadow on hero scripts. Every shape is a 0-radius rectangle; every rule is 1px.

07 — Do & Don't

Hold The Register

Do

  • Apply the radial surface, film grain, and hairline frame to every slide.
  • Set hero scripts in pink with the halo text-shadow.
  • Use DM Serif for editorial, Inter 300 for body, Mono for chrome.
  • Switch one headline word to pink via <em>; keep kickers pink.
  • Let one hero moment dominate with generous negative space.

Don't

  • Omit the film grain or interior frame, or add a box-shadow.
  • Introduce a second chromatic accent beyond pink.
  • Put body in DM Serif, or display in Inter.
  • Round any element, or use a 2px+ rule.
  • Crowd the slide with multiple equal-weight regions.
08 — 中文 / CJK

Couture, In Chinese

The couture identity rests on DM Serif Display's visual mass — matched in Chinese by 思源宋体 Noto Serif SC at weight 900 (anything lighter disappears against the pink-glow + dark paper). The pink halo and <em> switch transfer unchanged.
