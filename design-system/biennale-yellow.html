01 — Overview

Paper, Ink, and Yellow

No cards, no buttons, no shadows, no rounded corners. The structural vocabulary is just three things: warm parchment, deep-indigo ink, and solar yellow. The mood sits between a folded museum brochure, a slow literary quarterly, and a Mediterranean exhibition poster.

02 — Colours

One Yellow, One Ink

Warm parchment is the universal ground; deep-indigo ink is the single text and line colour; solar yellow is the signature — as a flooded panel, a soft radial bloom, or a translucent tile underprint. A subordinate ember peach balances the bloom. The system never inverts.

03 — Typography

Serif, Sans, Mono

Instrument Serif carries every display moment from 40px to 720px — weight 400 only, tight line-height, negative tracking. Archivo is body and the wide-tracked uppercase label. JetBrains Mono is reserved for dates and numerals.

05 — Layout

Editorial-Restrained

Elegant when sparse, broken when crowded. One large display moment plus a handful of supporting elements. Even dense surfaces achieve density through tabular repetition — many quiet hairline-separated rows, not many bordered cells. Generous 40–76px edge padding.

06 — Depth & Shape

Atmospheric, Not Structural

Zero drop shadows. Depth comes from layered radial blooms of yellow — the sun-bloom centred here — with an optional ember counter-bloom. Cards have no borders; regions separate only by 1px hairline rules. The lack of border weight is itself the aesthetic.

Sun bloom
42–70% radial, the primary depth.
Ember bloom
15–22%, opposite corner.
1px hairline
The only border, ever.
07 — Do & Don't

Confident Restraint

Do

  • Start every surface on warm parchment; add at least one sun-bloom.
  • Set every line of text in deep-indigo ink — one colour, full opacity.
  • Use Instrument Serif 400 with tight line-height and negative tracking.
  • Set labels in Archivo 600 uppercase with 0.16–0.32em tracking.
  • Reserve JetBrains Mono for dates, numerals, and page numbers.

Don't

  • Add drop shadows, round corners, or borders thicker than 1px.
  • Set Instrument Serif bold, or introduce a second text colour.
  • Invert — yellow or paper text on an ink ground does not exist.
  • Crowd the canvas with cards, buttons, or panel grids.
  • Omit the bloom — a flat parchment slide reads as a CMS template.
08 — 中文 / CJK

A Biennale, In Chinese

The closest register is 思源宋体 Noto Serif SC for editorial display and body, with 得意黑 Smiley Sans reserved for the loudest poster-scale moments. The single-ink, single-bloom discipline transfers cleanly — ink on paper, sun-bloom atmosphere, hairline rules.
