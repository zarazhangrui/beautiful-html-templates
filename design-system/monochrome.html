01 — Overview

A Single Material

Monochrome is a literary editorial system built on one constraint: black ink on cream paper. Eight tokens, but seven are tonal cream or graphite — there is no chromatic accent. The accent is simply darker ink. It reads as a carefully typeset monograph, where a single ultra-light Jost-200 headline can carry an otherwise nearly-empty page.

03 — Typography

Three Voices

Jost carries every display, headline, and body — the contrast between weight 200 and weight 300 is the system's primary rhythm. Lora italic appears in exactly two moments. JetBrains Mono handles every structural marker.

Jost 200 · the airy display voice
Jost · Display + Body
Geometric sans at weights 200 (display) / 300 (body) / 400 (sub). Calm at ultra-light; never heavier than 300 for headlines.
The only typographic warmth.
Lora · Italic Serif
Reserved for two moments only: pull-quote bodies and insight-card titles. Wherever it appears, it signals the human voice.
Structural Marker
JetBrains Mono · Chrome
Every label, sidebar tag, version number, axis, date, and bullet dash. Uppercase, tracked 0.12em or wider.

05 — Depth & Shape

No Shadow, Ever

Depth comes entirely from 1px hairline rules and generous whitespace. No drop shadows, no gradients, no elevation. When two regions need separation, a single black line divides them.

1px hairline rule
The only divider. Solid black, never dashed, never muted, never thicker.
36px short rule
A small punctuation mark between headline and lead — a visual pause.
Whitespace
8vw horizontal padding — the most generous in the library. Space is the elevation.
06 — Do & Don't

The Discipline

Do
  • Use cream paper on every slide — the single surface is the foundation.
  • Set headlines in Jost 200 and let weight and size do the work.
  • Reach for Lora italic only for pull-quotes and insight-card titles.
  • Render every divider as a 1px solid black rule.
  • Mark every bullet with a graphite mono em-dash.
  • Leave generous whitespace — middle 60–70% of the canvas.
Don't
  • Introduce any chromatic colour — the accent is darker ink.
  • Use Jost 500/600/700 for headlines — it reads as broken.
  • Put a pull-quote in sans — the Lora italic is its identity.
  • Use box-shadow on anything — there is no elevation.
  • Crowd a slide to the edges — content must breathe.
  • Swap the em-dash bullet for a dot, check, or arrow.
07 — 中文 · CJK

International Content

CJK cannot render at Jost-200 weight — it breaks. Display shifts to 思源黑体 Noto Sans SC 700; body to 400; quotes to 思源宋体 Noto Serif SC. Hierarchy comes from weight and size, never colour.

用户研究综合
