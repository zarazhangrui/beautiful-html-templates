§ 06 — DepthFlat with rounded chrome
a.
No shadows

No drop shadows, no gradients. Generous border-radius and saturated fills are the entire depth system.

b.
Solid hairlines

Inside cards, dividers are 1.5–2px solid warm-ink lines — heavier, because the system carries more visual weight.

c.
Dark alternate

A black field with bone type appears on agenda and bold-statement slides for contrast and gravity.

§ 07 — Do & Don'tThe register

Do

  • +Run Stardos Stencil for every headline and numeral — keep the ink-breaks.
  • +Let colour-block tablets do the layout; tile them geometrically.
  • +Run type huge — 120px statements, 220px numerals, 540px dividers.
  • +Use Barlow Condensed 800 uppercase for every label, pill, and legend.
  • +Keep card radii at 22–26px and dividers at 1.5–2px solid.

Don't

  • –Don't set body in the stencil face — Inter captions the loud type.
  • –Don't add drop shadows or gradients — depth is fill + radius only.
  • –Don't use pastel accents — they read as printed ink, not soft.
  • –Don't run timid type — scale is the system's primary expressive tool.
  • –Don't keep negative tracking on CJK — set it to zero.
Scale first · colour second · body last07 / 10
§ 08 — 中文 / CJKStrategy A

模板石板

Stardos Stencil 的墨断缺口与拉丁字母笔形绑定,中文无对应模板体 —— 整体替换为思源宋体 900(展示)、思源黑体重磅(chrome)、思源宋体(正文)。900 字重是唯一能在 120–540px 大尺度与 Stencil 视觉分量持平的权重。

每段中文都要做的调整

  • 整体换脸:Stardos 的墨断缺口只属于拉丁,中文用思源宋体 900,绝不强行让 Stencil 渲染汉字。
  • 混排标题(如 STUDIO 工作室)拆成两个 span:拉丁保留 Stardos,中文用宋体 900,字号调到视觉分量相当。
  • 放宽行高:展示 1.15–1.25(220–540px 汉字需要竖向呼吸),正文 1.75–1.85;字距一律归零。
  • 使用全角标点(,。:;「」),标题结尾不加句号;中英文边界加盘古之白:「2026 年」。
  • 色块 tablet、圆角、内描边、巨号尺度 —— 与语言无关,全部原样保留。
