Design System Specification

Cartesian

A quiet, museum-catalog editorial system — warm-stone neutrals, classical Playfair never bold, and a single 1px taupe line as the entire structural vocabulary. Drafting paper and compass work.

01 — Overview

Restraint Through 1px Lines

Every structural element — list separators, agenda rules, timeline connectors, card borders — is a single 1px line in muted taupe. No thick borders, no fills, no shadows. Hierarchy is built through type contrast and negative space. The reference is Vignelli, the Cooper Hewitt catalog, pencil-and-tracing-paper planning.

02 — Colours

Five Warm Stones, Plus Ink

There are no accent colours in the populist sense — no red, no blue, no green. The palette is stone, stone, stone, ink, ink. The only “colour” in the system is type contrast; emphasis comes from size, from serif-vs-sans, or from a single ink hairline.

03 — Typography

Serif Statements, Sans Structure

Playfair Display carries every headline, stat, and quote mark — at weight 400 only. The thin-stroke didone aesthetic depends on letting the letterforms breathe; bold Playfair does not exist here. Inter carries every body, label, and attribution.

05 — Layout

Sparse, And Breathing

Cartesian reads as elegant when sparse and broken when crowded. The correct density is “one clear idea, well-framed.” Most slides use centered or asymmetric two-column layouts with 5–6vw gutters and significant vertical breathing room.

4vh / 4vw default; 5–6vw column gutters.

Geometry

06 — Depth & Shape

A Single Flat Plane

No shadows, no elevated cards, no rounded surfaces, no gradients. Atmosphere is created by geometric line decorations — thin solid and dashed circles drifting behind content as if drawn with a compass on tracing paper, like the concentric ring centred on this slide. They create mood, not hierarchy.

07 — Do & Don't

Restraint Is The Message

Do

  • Use a single 1px taupe line for every structural separator.
  • Set every Playfair headline at weight 400 in ink.
  • Render labels & attributions in uppercase taupe with 2–3px tracking.
  • Layer one or two compass rings at 20–50% opacity for atmosphere.
  • Let slides breathe — sparse is elegant.

Don't

  • Introduce a populist accent colour — no red, blue, or green.
  • Bold any Playfair, or render headlines in taupe.
  • Add shadows, elevated cards, or gradient fills.
  • Round corners, or use borders thicker than 1px.
  • Crowd the canvas, or add more than two geo decorations.
08 — 中文 / CJK

A Museum Catalog, In Chinese

思源宋体 Noto Serif SC is the only mainstream Hanzi serif with the same restrained editorial register — its modulated strokes echo Playfair's didone. Use weight 700 for headlines, 400 for body, and never bold beyond that. The five-stone palette absorbs Hanzi cleanly.
