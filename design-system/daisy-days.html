01 · Overview

A Sunny Garden

Daisy Days descends from picture-book illustration and sticker-sheet kawaii. Every shape carries a charcoal 2D outline, every elevated element casts a solid offset shadow, and every surface is a pastel from a sunny garden palette. One focused content card per slide, surrounded by a wreath of corner ornaments.

02 · Colours

Pastels + One Pop

A multi-pastel garden over cream, with a single high-saturation coral pop reserved for small markers. Headlines on a colored surface get a 3px charcoal text-shadow; on cream they sit flat. Borders are always charcoal — never coloured.

05 · Depth & Shape

Sticker On Paper

Depth is 2D and graphic: a thick charcoal outline plus a solid charcoal offset shadow with zero blur. No gradients, no glow, no glass. Corners are always rounded — the smallest radius is 4px, most are 20px+.

06 · Do & Don't

Storybook Rules

Do
  • Pair Fredoka One headlines with Quicksand body, strictly by role.
  • Give every container a 3px charcoal outline + hard offset shadow.
  • Add the 3px text-shadow to headlines on colored surfaces.
  • Cluster 3–7 ornaments around every slide's edges.
  • Use outlined coloured dots as bullets — never bare hyphens.
  • Reserve coral for small markers only.
Don't
  • Use any third font, or cross the two face roles.
  • Use square corners — everything has radius.
  • Use blurred or rgba shadows — solid charcoal offsets only.
  • Use coloured borders — all borders are charcoal.
  • Flood a whole surface with coral.
  • Leave any corner empty of decorations.
07 · 中文 · CJK

International Content

Keep the storybook warmth in Chinese with 站酷小薇体 ZCOOL XiaoWei for headlines and 悠哉字体 Yozai for body. Keep the outlines, offset shadows, rounded corners, and ornament wreath — they carry the voice when the face changes.
