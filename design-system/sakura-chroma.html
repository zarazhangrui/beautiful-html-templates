The cassette.

§ 01 — Premise

Every slide is a printed product page from a small Japanese audio-products catalogue. The visual metaphor is total: petal-cluster blob marks, diagonal multi-colour ribbon bands, 12-point starburst seals, red rectangular stamps, mono-font spec rows, and equalizer-style bar charts.

Everything reads as if pulled from the back-page spread of a 1970s consumer-audio brochure — warm, hand-curated, industrially typeset.

The page.

§ 05 — Layout

A fluid 100vw × 100vh canvas with a generous frame inset. Section headers use the topbar-rule; every content slide carries a mono page number bottom-right.

Topbar rule

A Big Shoulders 900 title (with optional red em) aligned-end with a tracked-caps label, sealed by a 1.5px ink rule.

Frame inset

A generous edge inset frames the page; ribbons bleed off the opposite edge for the diagonal sweep.

Page number

Mono, formatted NN / TT, bottom-right — required on every content slide.

§ 06 — Depth
a.
Halftone grain

A 4px radial-dot texture at 16% anchors every flat colour block in the print register.

b.
Hard offset

The quote box carries a single hard 8px ink offset shadow — paper sitting on top of the ribbons.

c.
Ribbon depth

Diagonal colour ribbons sweep behind paper callouts, layering the page like a printed sleeve.

Warm but disciplined — playful but tightly typeset.

House rules.

§ 07 — Do & Don't

Do

  • +Keep the halftone-dot texture on every slide — it's the print register.
  • +Run display in Big Shoulders 900 condensed, in brown with red em-emphasis.
  • +Use the six primaries as bold flat blocks, ribbons, and topstrips.
  • +Fill checked boxes with a cream × — never a checkmark.
  • +Reach for ribbons, petal clusters, and starburst seals as anchors.

Don't

  • –Don't drop the grain — flat colour blocks lose the printed feel.
  • –Don't set body in the condensed display face — that's Albert Sans's job.
  • –Don't keep negative tracking on CJK — it collides the glyphs.
  • –Don't use soft or blurred shadows — depth is the single hard offset.
  • –Don't make petals ellipses — they're always perfect circles.

樱花色谱

展示与封印用站酷小薇体(优雅高对比的中文展示体,呼应 Big Shoulders 的竖向压缩与花瓣的柔美),正文用霞鹜文楷。两者都在 Google Fonts —— 切勿用 cn-fontsource 等 CDN。行内 em 变红/蓝的色彩强调在中文里同样成立。

每段中文都要做的调整

  • Big Shoulders 的负字距(-0.012 至 -0.025em)在中文必须归零 —— 站酷小薇本身已压缩,负字距会撞字。
  • 放宽行高:正文 1.75–1.85,展示 1.15–1.25;中文不做 uppercase。
  • 字距大写微标签无中文对应:中文标签字距归零,靠棕墨色 + 粗字重承载分类信号;中英混排时拉丁部分保留 0.16em+。
  • 使用全角标点(,。:;「」),标题结尾不加句号;中英文边界加盘古之白:「使用 Claude」。
  • 缎带、花瓣、星爆封印、红印章、彩色顶条 —— 与语言无关,全部原样保留。
