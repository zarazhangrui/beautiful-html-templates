03 — TypographyPlayfair · Jost · Mono
Never Bold

Playfair 400, italicised in coral

Playfair Display at weight 400 carries every serif moment — bold is not permitted. The signature move is an em switching to italic coral. Jost 300 is the "good paper" body; JetBrains Mono 300 the chrome voice.

07 — Do & Don'tHold the silence
The Discipline

Hold the silence

Do

  • Run every serif at Playfair 400 — never bold.
  • Use <em> for italic-coral emphasis inside headlines.
  • Pair the kicker with the 36px coral rule as one unit.
  • Use the em-dash in coral as the only bullet glyph.
  • Keep slides spacious; add space rather than content.

Don't

  • Use bold serif, or a fourth typeface.
  • Use coral as a fill or for body paragraphs.
  • Add box-shadow, gradient, blur, or thick borders.
  • Round corners, or use round bullets / hyphens.
  • Crowd the canvas — Grove rewards silence.
08 — 中文 / CJKStrategy C · LXGW WenKai
Literary, In Chinese

Hand-set warmth

Keep Playfair for Latin; let Chinese fall through to 霞鹜文楷 LXGW WenKai, whose hand-set warmth mirrors Playfair 400. Chinese has no italic, so the <em> accent becomes colour-only coral.
