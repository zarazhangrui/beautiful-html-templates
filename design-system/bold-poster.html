01 — Overview

The System

Bold Poster lifts its vocabulary from vintage Italian sports magazines, mid-century European brand annual reports, and wine-merchant catalogues. Every slide should feel printed — set in heavy display type, locked to one strong red accent, on a white or off-white sheet, with grids ruled in ink and decoration kept to a strict minimum.

02 — Colours

Four Colours, No More

Uncompromising restraint: a white canvas, deep brown-black ink, a single saturated tomato red, and a warm off-white for alternating panels. No secondary brand colours, no gradients, no semantic state colours. Every figure, every active rule, every emphasis moment is red — and red is reserved for those moments only.

#FFFFFF
Pure white canvas. The default ground for most surfaces. Reads as fresh newsprint paper.

Deep brown-black with warm bias — not pure black. Every line of body, every border, every label.

Saturated tomato red. The single accent: every numeral, every eyebrow, every leftbar, every bullet, the progress bar.

Warm off-white. Alternating panel backgrounds for vertical striping; subtly warmer than the white canvas.
03 — Typography

Three Faces, Strict Roles

The roles are non-overlapping. Shrikhand handles every display moment and every numeral; Libre Baskerville handles every body paragraph; Space Grotesk handles every uppercase label, bullet, and chrome element. Don't cross — Shrikhand body would be illegible, Space Grotesk headlines would feel like a tech startup.

Aa 47%
Shrikhand · Display + Numerical
Single-weight (400) heavy slab-script with playful italic terminals. Used 18px–320px, almost always rotated. Carries every hero title, section header, stat figure, card title.
The literary serif body voice.
Libre Baskerville · Body
Classical literary serif with high stroke contrast. Set 11–16px at 1.5–1.75 line-height in deep ink — what makes the system feel printed rather than digital.
Stamped Metadata
Space Grotesk · Chrome
Used 9–12px in uppercase with 2–3px tracking for labels, eyebrows, counters, links, and card bullets. The system's "stamped metadata" voice.

06 — Depth & Shapes

Depth Without Shadow

The system has zero box-shadows. Depth is structural: heavy borders, surface inversion, and tilted display that breaks the grid. Corners are square everywhere (0px) — a rounded card collapses the printed-poster feel into generic web.

07 — Do & Don't

Hold The Line

Do
  • Set every hero as a multi-line Shrikhand stack with at least one rotated line and one red line.
  • Use red Shrikhand for every numeral — it's the system's data voice.
  • Set every eyebrow in Space Grotesk 600 uppercase, 2–3px tracking, red.
  • Build grids with the 3px + 1.5px double border.
  • Render every bullet as a red em-dash or round-bullet at position absolute left.
  • Keep Libre Baskerville body at line-height 1.5–1.75.
Don't
  • Introduce a second accent colour — no green, blue, or yellow.
  • Round any corner (except the 4px hint-pill chrome).
  • Add drop shadows or blurred shadows of any kind.
  • Substitute fonts — the trio is the identity.
  • Use default disc bullets, ever.
  • Omit the tilt on hero-class red Shrikhand — untilted reads as flat.
08 — 中文 / CJK

International Content

Shrikhand has no Chinese equivalent, so the three-face trio collapses to two: 思源宋体 Noto Serif SC (weight 900 display / 400 body) and 思源黑体 Noto Sans SC for chrome. Hierarchy shifts from face-contrast to weight + colour + rotation — and the non-typographic signatures (tilt, red, double-border, leftbar) carry the voice.

大胆海报
我们不追逐市场，我们建造市场赖以运转的基础设施。思源宋体 900 承载编辑重量与气场，旋转与饱和红保留了海报的运动感。盘古之白：在中文与 Latin 之间插入空格。
