01 — OverviewStudio / Design System

The Headline Is The Design

Studio is a type-as-graphic-mass system — the register of contemporary design-studio decks. A single typeface, a single weight, strict uppercase, at scales so large that type stops behaving like type and starts behaving like a shape. Remove everything but the headline and the slide still reads as Studio.

02 — ColoursBinary + opacity

Binary, Plus One

The entire system is dark / yellow, yellow / dark. The same acid yellow is the type on dark slides and the background of light slides. No third colour, no greys — muted text is just the same colour at reduced opacity.

One Face, One Weight

Barlow · Content (display → body)
Graphic Mass
A contemporary grotesque. At weight 900, display scale, the counters become structural negative space and the headline reads as a block before it reads as words. Carries every headline, numeral, quote, lead and body line.

05 — Layout100vw × 100vh · tight pad

Tight By Design

Padding runs tighter than its peers (5vw / 5vh) precisely so display type runs to the near-edge — the heading is the spatial fill, not the margin. Standard slides carry a top chrome bar and a mirrored foot bar; cover, chapter, statement and quote slides go chromeless.

06 — Depth & ShapeFlat · severe

07 — Do & Don'tHold the register

Hold The Register

Do
  • Set every headline in Barlow 900 uppercase with at least -0.01em tracking.
  • Alternate near-black and acid-yellow surfaces freely — both are first-class.
  • Colour every headline in the surface accent: yellow on dark, near-black on yellow.
  • Use IBM Plex Mono only for metadata; mute secondary text with opacity, not grey.
  • Apply 2px rules to stat-tops, baselines, and dividers; 1px to chrome.
  • Pad tight and leave statement slides deliberately sparse.
Don't
  • Lowercase a headline, or use weight 800 / 700 at display scale.
  • Add a third colour — the palette is binary plus opacity.
  • Round any corner, add a drop shadow, or introduce a gradient.
  • Use Barlow for chrome metadata, or Mono for headlines / body.
  • Add italic, underline, or colour variants for emphasis.
  • Fill more than ~60% of a slide; crowding reads as broken.

08 — 中文 / CJKStrategy A · replace, not stack

Woodblock, Not Signage

Barlow 900 has no CJK coverage, so Chinese elements replace the entire font-family with 思源宋体 Noto Serif SC — never a stack fallback (the metric mismatch breaks the single-weight register). The "type-as-graphic-mass" effect survives, but shifts from industrial signage toward Chinese woodblock / letterpress poster.
