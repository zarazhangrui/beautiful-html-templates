Overview

i. — the premise

Every visible mark — every headline, paragraph, border, rule, pill, and page number — is rendered in a single warm rust terracotta on buttery cream. The only chromatic variation is opacity. By committing to one ink, the system earns the calm authority of a printed object.

The aesthetic borrows from one-colour screen printing, letterpress posters, and Risograph stationery — formats where each additional colour is a separate printing pass and therefore a deliberate decision.

Layout

v. — fluid stage

A 100vw × 100vh canvas with clamp() units throughout — the same composition renders correctly on a laptop, a monitor, and a 1440p display without media queries.

Topbar divider

A Bricolage headline beside a small italic Fraunces label, sealed below by a 1.5px ink rule — the universal page divider.

Page number

Italic Fraunces, bottom-right of every slide. The marker is the system's spine.

Nav hint

A faint italic Fraunces string at the bottom-left, at 45% opacity — the interactivity affordance.

Border weights

1.5px structural outlines, 1px @ 32% solid or dashed internal dividers. Nothing thicker.

Depth

vi. — flat, printed
i.
Outlines

Elevation is communicated through 1.5px solid ink borders on cards, badges, pills, and tags — never a fill, never a shadow.

ii.
Opacity rules

1px solid or 1px dashed internal dividers at 32% opacity carry the structure inside cards and between ledger rows.

iii.
Paper texture

A 4px radial-dot overlay at 10% — invisible at distance, present up close. The whole system reads as ink soaked into paper.

Do & Don't

vii. — the register

Do

  • i.Commit to single-ink rendering — use opacity for variation, never a different hue.
  • ii.Apply the 4px radial-dot paper texture to every slide.
  • iii.Run Bricolage uppercase 700–800 with negative tracking; Fraunces italic by default.
  • iv.Reach for the italic Fraunces jumbo numeral as the cover anchor.
  • v.Pair every edition badge with its italic "Edition N." label.

Don't

  • i.Don't introduce a second ink colour — it shatters the printed-program register.
  • ii.Don't fill any shape — cards, pills, badges, tags are outline-only.
  • iii.Don't use box-shadow, gradient, or blur. The system is flat printed paper.
  • iv.Don't run Bricolage in sentence case, or Fraunces roman by default.
  • v.Don't use medium radii (4–12px) — only 999px, 50%, or 0.
