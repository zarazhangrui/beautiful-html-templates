The premise

Received

Every slide is yellow legal-pad paper. A base colour, two soft radial highlights, and a non-optional fractal-noise grain make a surface that reads as physical paper under raking light. Three non-overlapping voices carry the rest.

Depth

§ 06

A single signature mechanism: the hard offset shadow — a solid ink-blue rectangle offset down-and-right with zero blur. No soft shadows, no glow.

a.
Hard offset

Every card carries a 5–6px solid ink-blue shadow with zero blur. Cards look pinned and slightly lifted.

b.
Rotation

Slight 0.6–1.5° rotation on alternate cards makes the page feel hand-assembled, not gridded.

c.
Paper grain

The fractal-noise overlay is the atmosphere — the whole page reads as physical stock under light.

Do & Don't

§ 07

Do

  • ✓Keep the fractal-noise grain on every slide — it's foundational, not decorative.
  • ✓Give every card the hard ink-blue offset shadow, 1.5px border, and 4px radius.
  • ✓Use the safety pin to "pin" cards — always rotated off-axis.
  • ✓Keep the three voices in their lanes: Grotesk print, Caveat margin, Mono tag.
  • ✓Reserve red for the stamp and the negative pill only.

Don't

  • ✕Don't drop the grain — flat cartoon-yellow collapses the system.
  • ✕Don't use soft or blurred shadows — the offset is hard, zero blur.
  • ✕Don't set Caveat at body length, or Space Grotesk in the margins.
  • ✕Don't use red as a text colour, fill, or border outside its two roles.
  • ✕Don't place pins perfectly upright — the slight rotation is the craft.

Pin & Paper 是全库最契合中文的系统:黄纸、钴蓝墨、安全别针、田野笔记气质在中文里同样成立。展示与批注都用龙藏体(硬笔手写),正文用霞鹜文楷 —— 别针、印章、卡片硬投影全部原样保留。

每段中文都要做的调整

  • 声部区分靠字号、旋转、颜色而非换字体:龙藏体 38px 旋转 -3° 在边上即读作批注,96px 正排即读作标题。
  • 放宽行高:正文 1.75–1.85,展示 1.15–1.25;字距归零;中文不做 uppercase。
  • 使用全角标点(,。:;「」),标题结尾不加句号;中英文边界加盘古之白:「使用 Claude」。
  • DM Mono 不转中文:中文元数据用霞鹜文楷正排,纯拉丁(日期、来源)仍保留 DM Mono。
  • 别针、红印章、奶油卡 + 蓝墨硬投影 —— 与语言无关,完全不变。
