Playful

A warm, hand-crafted editorial system — peach-clay canvas, charcoal ink, double-stroke borders, organic blobs, and scribbled doodles. Studio sketchbook, not corporate pitch.

01 — Overview

Ink On Clay Paper

Playful commits fully to a one-colour discipline: charcoal ink on a peach-clay canvas, nothing else. Expressiveness comes from shape, weight, rotation, and hand-drawn marks — not from palette variety. Confident but warm; structured but loose.

03 — Typography

Expressive + Reliable

Syne is the personality — a quirky humanist display sans at weight 700–800 with tight negative tracking. Space Grotesk is the workhorse — steady, neutral, at 400–500. The rhythm is Syne headline + Space Grotesk body, repeated with discipline.

05 — Layout

One Moment, Breathing

Each slide is anchored by a single dominant element — a statement, a chart, a roster — surrounded by deliberate breathing room and one or two scribbles. The system reads as expensive when sparse and broken when dense.

Padding & gap
pad-slide 3–5rem · pad-card 1.5–3rem · gap 1.5 / 2 / 3rem. Generous on cover & closing; tighter inside cards.
One dominant element
A single substantive moment per slide, plus one or two SVG scribbles in the negative space. Never wall-to-wall.
06 — Depth & Shape

Drawn, Not Rendered

No web shadows. Depth is the double-stroke offset border, small ±3° rotations (alternating direction), and ink-density contrast. Corners are sharp, perfectly round, or organically blob-shaped — never a generic medium radius.

07 — Do & Don't

Keep It Hand-Touched

Do
  • Use Syne for display, Space Grotesk for body; negative-track every Syne element.
  • Apply the double-stroke offset border to every primary card.
  • Keep charcoal as the only ink; reach for bg-alt for a distinct surface.
  • Add a scribble to a corner of every slide; rotate cards ±0.5–3°, alternating.
  • Let one dominant element breathe; reserve the inverted card for the anchor.
Don't
  • Introduce a third colour or a chromatic chart palette.
  • Use blurred box-shadow, or medium radii (4/8/12px).
  • Set headlines in Space Grotesk, or body in Syne.
  • Rotate past ±3°, or rotate every element the same direction.
  • Crowd the canvas, or use italic / underline for emphasis.
08 — 中文 / CJK

Warm, In Chinese

The transfer is exceptionally strong: 站酷快乐体 ZCOOL KuaiLe carries the same quirky independent-studio voice as Syne, and 悠哉字体 Yozai mirrors Space Grotesk's warmth. Drop the negative tracking on CJK display — the rounded faces are designed on a square em-box.
