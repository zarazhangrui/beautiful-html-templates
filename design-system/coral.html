01 — Overview

Solid Color Planes

The defining premise is solid colour planes meeting at hard edges: a single slide splits into coral + cream + ink regions, each a flat surface holding a self-contained composition. There are no gradient transitions — the meeting point of two colours is the layout. A sports-magazine cover, not a literary catalogue.

02 — Colours

Three Surfaces, One Fire

03 — Typography

Bebas Declares, Inter Explains

Bebas Neue — tall condensed caps, single weight 400, heavy 1–12px tracking, always uppercase — carries every headline, stat, and title as an architectural element. Inter carries every body, label, and attribution across weights 300–700.

05 — Layout

Region Splits

The primary move is multi-surface composition — slides split into regions of solid colour, the boundary between them defining the layout. Each region holds a self-contained composition, fully populated within its boundary.

06 — Depth & Shape

Flat, Hard Edges

No drop shadows, no rounded surfaces, no soft elevations. Hierarchy comes from hard colour region boundaries, accent borders (5px coral top, 4px coral left), the 45° hatch, and oversized decorative typography. Only nav dots and timeline nodes are circles.

07 — Do & Don't

Hold The Fire

Do

  • Compose slides as multi-surface region splits meeting at hard edges.
  • Set every Bebas element uppercase with 1–12px letter-spacing.
  • Render headlines ink-on-coral — never white on coral.
  • Apply the 45° hatch to coral regions; add 12% wallpaper numerals.
  • Use the 5px coral top / 4px coral left borders as the only card chrome.

Don't

  • Render Bebas in sentence case, or without letter-spacing.
  • Introduce a fourth surface colour or a second accent.
  • Add drop shadows, rounded rectangles, or soft gradients.
  • Render headlines in gray, or pair Bebas with another body sans.
  • Leave a coral region sparse — fill it or add wallpaper type.
08 — 中文 / CJK

Poster, In Chinese

站酷小薇体 ZCOOL XiaoWei carries the architectural-display register Bebas provides for Latin; 悠哉字体 Yozai mirrors Inter's warmth. Bebas's "uppercase + tracking" rule is meaningless for Hanzi — drop both and let the face stand on size alone.
