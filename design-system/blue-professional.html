01 — OverviewConsulting-grade

Restraint, With One Commitment

A warm cream canvas and a single saturated cobalt that carries every accent, every metric, every CTA, every eyebrow, every chart fill. No secondary brand colour, no pastels — just cream, cobalt, and a tight ladder of muted grays. Borrowed from investment-research reports and McKinsey-grade briefings.

02 — ColoursOne accent

Cream, Cobalt, Three Grays

Cobalt does all the emphasis work — headlines stay near-black, but every metric, eyebrow, CTA, chart bar, and progress indicator is cobalt. Borders are cobalt at 20%, card fills cobalt at 4%. The only non-cobalt accents are a subdued green and red, used inline on directional change indicators only.

03 — TypographyTwo faces

Space Grotesk + Inter

Space Grotesk handles every heading, numeral, and chrome element; Inter handles every paragraph. The pairing is deliberately ordinary — what makes it distinctive is the discipline of usage. Headlines are near-black; cobalt is reserved for eyebrows, numerals, and CTAs.

06 — Depth & ShapeSoft & tinted

Tint, Not Shadow

Cards are 4% cobalt tints with 1.5px cobalt-at-20% borders and 10–14px rounded corners. There are zero drop shadows except a single soft cobalt CTA hover. Every elevation is implied by border + tint, not by shadow. Soft rounded corners are part of the depth language.

07 — Do & Don'tDiscipline

Restraint Is The Identity

Do
  • Use cream ground + cobalt as the only accent across the whole deck.
  • Set headlines near-black; reserve cobalt for eyebrows, metrics, CTAs.
  • Use tinted cards: 4% fill, 20% border, 10–14px radius — never solid.
  • Pair every slide-header with an eyebrow (left) and tag pill (right).
  • Render numerals in cobalt Space Grotesk; body in muted Inter.
Don't
  • Introduce a second accent colour, or set headlines in cobalt.
  • Use drop shadows, or opaque cobalt borders.
  • Use square (0px) corners anywhere except the progress bar.
  • Substitute the Space Grotesk + Inter pairing.
  • Use the green/red chips for non-directional emphasis.
08 — 中文 / CJKStrategy A

Consulting, In Chinese

思源黑体 Noto Sans SC carries every Space Grotesk role; 思源宋体 Noto Serif SC carries body — the sans-display + serif-body pairing reinforces the consulting-grade editorial register common in Chinese investor reports. The cobalt discipline transfers entirely.

蓝色专业体系
暖奶油底色，单一钴蓝作为唯一强调色，柔和的钴蓝淡色卡片（4% 填充 + 20% 描边 + 圆角，零投影）。标题用近黑色，钴蓝只用于眉标、数字与 CTA。中文 letter-spacing 归零，眉标搭配钴蓝短线以补足大写字距的识别作用。盘古之白：2024 Q3 业绩复盘。
