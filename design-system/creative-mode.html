Restraint Of
Technique

Restraint of technique paired with aggression of expression: no rounded cards, no gradients, no light-source shadows — only flat colour-blocking and hard offset drop-shadows. Four accents fire at full saturation; they never blend, they collide, and the collision is the design.

07 — Do & Don'tRules
The Discipline

Loud, Not Noisy

Do
  • Use 4px ink borders on every structural element.
  • Keep Archivo Black uppercase with 0.92 line-height.
  • Use two or three accents per slide, never all four.
  • Hard offset shadows in exactly two sizes: 24px, 18px.
  • Reserve the green background for a single closing slide.
Don't
  • Round card corners (except the topbar pill).
  • Use gradients, blurred shadows, or glow effects.
  • Lowercase Archivo Black, or add letter-spacing.
  • Introduce a fifth accent, or use pure white.
  • Use Space Grotesk for labels — that's mono's job.
08 — 中文 / CJKStrategy A
Brutalist, In Chinese

Heavy Serif Mass

Archivo Black's punk-zine density has no exact Hanzi match, so 思源宋体 900 is the heaviest substitute. The four-colour blocks, 4px borders, and hard offset shadows do most of the brutalist work — the type becomes one loud component, not the whole system.
