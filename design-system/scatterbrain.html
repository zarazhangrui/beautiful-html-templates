01 — Overview

A Designer's Whiteboard

Every content block is a colored sticky note pinned to cork, desk-paper, or warm-gradient surfaces, layered with thumbtacks, tape, and doodles. The metaphor is total: the deck is a brainstorm wall, and the content is the cluster of notes pinned across it.

05 — Layout

Pinned, Not Gridded

Notes sit on one of three textured backgrounds with small rotations and corner doodles. Composition is loose: a centered manifesto note, a 2–3 column grid, a free cluster, or alternating timeline rows. Density is 1–4 main notes plus 1–2 accents.

06 — Depth & Shape

Soft Lift, Hand-Placed

The one system in the library that embraces soft blurred shadows — every note lifts off the surface with a 15px-blur drop shadow. Add small rotations (alternating direction), thumbtacks, and tape for tactile layering.

07 — Do & Don't

Keep It Hand-Placed

Do
  • Pick a background variant per slide; keep the grain overlay on every one.
  • Use Shrikhand for display, Zilla Slab for body, Caveat for scribbled notes.
  • Give every note a small rotation; alternate directions across neighbours.
  • Pin every primary note; combine pin + tape on hero / statement notes.
  • Drop 1–2 corner doodles at 0.15 opacity, and cycle note colours.
Don't
  • Let notes float on plain white — the textured backgrounds are the ground.
  • Swap Shrikhand or Caveat for another face, or use Zilla for headlines.
  • Assign semantic meaning to note colours — they're categorical only.
  • Rotate past ±15°, or rotate every note the same direction.
  • Crowd the canvas with overlapping notes, or skip the pin on a headline.
08 — 中文 / CJK

Playful, In Chinese

Match the playful-tactile register: 站酷快乐体 ZCOOL KuaiLe echoes Shrikhand's chunky marker-pen voice; 悠哉字体 Yozai mirrors Zilla Slab's warm slab. Caveat has no CJK equivalent — keep handwritten asides in Latin as margin notes, a common convention in Chinese creative decks.
