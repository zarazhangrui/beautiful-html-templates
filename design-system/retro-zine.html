The premise

№ 01

Retro Zine borrows from independent press culture, mid-century activist posters, and DIY zine layouts: condensed industrial display type, hand-script emphasis, slightly rotated stamps, masking-tape collage marks, drop caps, and a print-grain overlay that ties every surface to a printed-paper register.

Three faces carry distinct emotional roles — Bebas Neue is the display voice, Space Grotesk the body, and Caveat the hand-script. Together they read as an editorial spread typeset by a small press.

The page

№ 05

A fluid 100vw × 100vh canvas with 60px padding. 3px solid black borders divide regions; 2px and 1.5px borders subdivide. Density is medium-high — the zine voice expects a layout packed with editorial moves.

A solid green block with cream text — section labels, accent strips, inline highlight bars.

House rules

№ 07

Do

  • ✓Keep the SVG grain overlay on every slide at 7%.
  • ✓Run Bebas Neue uppercase, tracked, for every display moment.
  • ✓Use the green offset slab behind cards — depth without box-shadow.
  • ✓Rotate stamps and collage pieces for the hand-placed feel.
  • ✓Reserve Caveat for attributions, side notes, and RSVP writing.

Don't

  • ✕Don't drop the grain — flat colour loses the printed register.
  • ✕Don't use blurred drop shadows — depth is the offset slab.
  • ✕Don't set body or headlines in the hand-script face.
  • ✕Don't introduce a third hue — khaki + green + ink only.
  • ✕Don't centre one lonely headline — the zine voice expects density.

中文 / CJK

№ 08

复古独立刊

Bebas Neue 的压缩工业大写无中文对应,整体改用思源宋体:900 字重的深衬线笔形呼应活版与木刻印刷,比任何中文黑体都更贴近这套中世纪活动家海报气质。读作「同一本刊物的中文版」,而非两套打架的系统。Caveat 手写仅用于拉丁。

Caveat 无中文替代

每段中文都要做的调整

  • Bebas 的 0.02–0.04em 正字距在中文必须归零 —— 字距会把每个字视觉上拆成独立词,只靠 900 字重承载展示分量。
  • 展示行高从 0.85–0.95 放宽到 1.15–1.25;正文 1.75–1.85;汉字占满字框,需要竖向呼吸。
  • 中文不做 uppercase;使用全角标点(,。:;「」),标题结尾不加句号。
  • 中英文边界加盘古之白:「使用 Claude」「2026 年」。
  • 绿色偏移卡、胶带、印章旋转、3px 黑边、颗粒覆盖 —— 与语言无关,全部原样保留。
