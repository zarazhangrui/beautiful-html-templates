03 — TypographyBricolage · DM Sans · DM Mono
Always Mixed Case

Heavy display, light body

Bricolage Grotesque at 700–800 is the display voice — always mixed case, never uppercase. DM Sans 400 is the body; DM Mono carries every label and the uppercase work. The wide weight gap is deliberate.

06 — Depth & ShapeAtmospheric, not stacked
The Glow

Depth from light, not shadow

The only depth is the wood-brown radial glow anchored to the bottom-right corner of every dark slide — you can see it warming this corner right now. No drop shadows, no rounded corners, no elevation. Region separation is 1px hairlines only.

07 — Do & Don'tKeep it warm & slow
The Discipline

Keep it warm & slow

Do
  • Keep the wood-brown glow on every dark slide — it's part of the surface.
  • Set Bricolage display in mixed case; reserve uppercase for mono labels.
  • Use burnt orange only inline — kicker, em-dash, <em>, one chart bar.
  • Use the cream info-card to inset warm paper on the green field.
  • Keep slides medium-sparse with generous side padding.
Don't
  • Uppercase any Bricolage display, or add a second accent colour.
  • Use orange as a background or a large headline colour.
  • Add drop shadows, rounded corners, or borders on the info-card.
  • Use a serif face, or italic letterforms.
  • Crowd the slide with three columns of bullets.
08 — 中文 / CJKMincho warmth
Warm Material, In Chinese

All-Mincho, one accent

Mat's identity is environmental, not face-specific. Switch the whole stack to 思源宋体 Noto Serif SC — its warm Mincho letterforms match the wood-glow / forest-green register better than a geometric sans. The glow, orange accent, and info-card all transfer unchanged.
