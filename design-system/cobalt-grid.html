The Premise

A two-colour risograph monograph: cream paper, electric cobalt ink, and a graph-paper grid that lives permanently behind every slide. The reference is WIRED Japan, Shift magazine, and architectural trend reports printed in two-colour riso — cream + one cobalt.

The Grid Wants Filling

The top and bottom 1.5px cobalt hairlines frame every slide; the page number sits above the bottom hairline, the nav hint mirrors it. Index, data, and table layouts are dense; manifesto, quote, and colophon leave the grid breathing.

Depth & ShapeFLAT / 06
Flat, With Structural Rules

No drop shadows, no elevated cards, no rounded surfaces. Depth is the 1.5px cobalt rules, the 1px faint dividers, the 10% graph-paper grid, and the pixel-glitch texture. Border-radius is 0 everywhere — the system has zero circular elements.

Do & Don'tRULES / 07
Do
  • Keep the graph-paper grid behind every slide — it is the canvas tone.
  • Set every Newsreader element at weight 400 in cobalt.
  • Render labels in Hanken 600 uppercase with 0.16em+ tracking.
  • Use pixel-glitch columns + QR patches as the signature decoration.
  • Render charts as pixel-stack grid cells (cobalt on / 10% off).
Don't
  • Introduce a second ink colour — the system is strictly two-colour.
  • Bold a Newsreader headline, or disable the grid.
  • Round any corner — there are zero circular elements.
  • Add drop shadows, elevated cards, or gradients.
  • Use Hanken caps without 0.16em+ tracking.
