01 — Overview

Five Structural Laws

BlockFrame is a maximalist neobrutalist system: every region has a 4px black border, every elevated element an 8px hard offset shadow, every corner is square, every accent a saturated pastel, and every layout is allowed to be a little crooked. The joy comes from the deliberate collision of these laws.

03 — Typography

Heavy Caps, Wide Chrome

Inter at weight 800–900 in tight uppercase with negative tracking is the display voice; Space Grotesk at weight 600 uppercase with 0.08em tracking is the label voice. The contrast between heavy display and wide-tracked chrome is the whole rhythm.

07 — Do & Don't

Keep It Chunky

Do

  • Pair every 4px border with an 8px shadow, every 3px with a 4px shadow.
  • Cycle slide grounds through the five pastels for rhythm.
  • Set Inter display uppercase, weight 800+, negative tracking.
  • Use the label-pill as the universal section eyebrow.
  • Tilt decorations ±2° to ±12°, and add a decorative unit to every surface.

Don't

  • Round any corner (except the stat-deco dot) or blur any shadow.
  • Use colored borders — black only, except the white close-frame.
  • Set Inter display in sentence case or without negative tracking.
  • Introduce a sixth pastel, or omit the label-pill eyebrow.
  • Keep everything perfectly aligned — the tilt is the energy.
08 — 中文 / CJK

Structural, Not Typographic

The system is 80% structural, 20% typographic — so CJK survives. Strategy A: one family, 思源黑体 Noto Sans SC, with built-in Latin coverage. Hierarchy comes from weight (900 display / 600 label / 400 body), since the uppercase + negative-tracking signal disappears in CJK.

4px 黑边、8px 硬投影、五色糖果底色逐屏循环 —— 这些结构性元素承担了野兽派的全部识别度，与字母是否大写无关。思源黑体 900 在同样的大尺寸下，配合黑边与硬投影，依然读作野兽派。盘古之白：在中文与 Latin 之间留一个空格，如 BlockFrame 设计系统、2026 年。
