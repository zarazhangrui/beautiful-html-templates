The Premise

A long-form briefing that runs as a deck

The deck does not need to shout because the typography already telegraphs seriousness. A dual-surface model — deep editorial navy and warm aged paper — connected by a single antique gold, used only on rules, italic emphasis, and figures.

02 — ColoursDual surface + one accent
The Palette

Two surfaces, one hot accent

Navy and cream are interchangeable — both first-class, alternating freely. Exactly one accent: antique gold, on rules, italic emphasis, and numerals. Gold never fills a background, never carries body.

03 — TypographyVoice · substance · timestamp
The Signal Moment

Roman, then italic gold, mid-sentence

Serif = voice, sans = substance, mono = timestamp. The most distinctive move is mixing roman and italic Source Serif inside one headline, the italic always in gold. An em inside a sans paragraph switches to italic serif gold — the font change is the emphasis.

05 — LayoutBreathing room
The Frame

One thing, said carefully

A typical slide uses a small fraction of its real estate — a kicker, a headline, a sentence, a footer. Standard slides carry a chrome bar and foot bar; cover, chapter, statement, and quote slides go chromeless. 7.5vw / 5.5vh padding gives the editorial breathing room.

06 — Depth & ShapeFlat plus hairline
No Elevation

Flat, plus a single hairline

No drop shadows, no rounded chrome, no elevation. Regions separate via 1px hairlines. The only texture is the 80px grid on dark slides at 3% white — the system's fingerprint. Corners are square everywhere except the donut chart.

07 — Do & Don'tHold the restraint
The Discipline

Hold the restraint

Do
  • Mix roman + italic-gold Source Serif inside one headline — the Signal moment.
  • Alternate navy and cream surfaces freely; both are first-class.
  • Colour every numeral and kicker gold; use em-dash gold bullets.
  • Separate regions with 1px hairlines; keep the 80px grid on dark slides.
  • Pad generously — restraint is the register.
Don't
  • Use gold on body or as a background fill.
  • Use pure white on navy — always the warm off-white.
  • Add drop shadows, rounded chrome, or a second accent.
  • Put serif on body, or bold body text for emphasis.
  • Fill more than half a slide — crowding breaks the system.
08 — 中文 / CJKColor-only Signal moment
Editorial Bilingualism

The Signal moment, color-only

Noto Serif SC has no italic axis, so the roman→italic move becomes a color-only shift to gold in Chinese. The serif/sans contrast softens, but hairlines, mono kickers, gold accents, and dual surfaces carry the editorial restraint through.
