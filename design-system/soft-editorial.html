Soft Editorial

a warm magazine spread, set in Cormorant Garamond

Cream paper, deep ink, and a quartet of pastel candy accents on generous rounded cards. The mood is editorial calm with a sprinkling of riso-print colour — closer to a literary research notebook than a corporate deck.

i. — The premise

One serif, doing
nearly all the talking.

Soft Editorial takes its cues from small-press literary quarterlies and design-research notebooks. The cream paper field is the constant — every slide sits on a warm aged cream that reads as physical paper, not screen white. On top of the field, rounded cards float in two registers: translucent white softness for default content, and saturated pastel candy for accent moments.

The typography is single-family with mixed style. Headlines are Cormorant Garamond medium, but a roman headline often carries an italic phrase mid-sentence that drops to weight 400 — gentle emphasis, the same family, a lighter voice.

iii. — Single-family, mixed style

The weight drop is
the gentle emphasis.

Soft Editorial

Cormorant Garamond at weight 500 carries every headline; an italic phrase drops to weight 400 mid-sentence. Italic also runs every small ornamental moment — kickers, markers, page numerals, footers, step numerals, sign-offs. Work Sans steps in only for body and eyebrows, where the serif's personality would tire. Never mono, never uppercase, sentence case throughout.

vi. — Translucency, not elevation

Depth from form,
never from shadow.

i.
Translucent cards

White at 55% lets the warm paper bleed through, making the card feel lifted without any shadow.

ii.
Rounded form

Every card is at least 22px rounded, which reads as a physical card rather than a screen rectangle.

iii.
No second tier

Cards never stack on cards. Where a layout needs hierarchy, it uses size and colour — not z-stacking.

Dashed hairlines reinforce the notebook feelvi

vii. — Hold the register

Quiet, warm, unhurried.

Do

  • i.Use Cormorant 500 for headlines, dropping italic em phrases to weight 400.
  • ii.Keep the slide on cream — pastels are card fills, not backgrounds.
  • iii.Keep text ink on every surface, including all pastel cards.
  • iv.Use italic serif for kickers, markers, page numerals, and footers.
  • v.Use lowercase Roman numerals (i. ii. iii.) for step ordinals.

Don't

  • i.Don't fill a slide background with pastel (except the closer's pink moment).
  • ii.Don't invert text to white on pastel cards.
  • iii.Don't use square corners — the minimum radius is 14px.
  • iv.Don't add drop shadows, a third typeface, or any mono.
  • v.Don't use uppercase outside the 11px swatch label.
Sentence case throughoutvii

每一段中文都要做的调整

  • 正文行高放到 1.75–1.85,展示放到 1.15–1.25 —— 文楷笔画需要更多空气;字距设为 0,负字距会让汉字重叠。
  • 正文从 Work Sans 切到思源宋体:黑体正文配文楷标题在中文里像廉价教科书,宋体的宋朝笔形才能保住文学气质。
  • 首字下沉不适用于中文 —— 文楷的首字会显得被「切断」;中文开篇改用上方的斜体题引带出。
  • 斜体强调在中文不成立(文楷无真斜体):改用 ink-soft 颜色或淡下划线,绝不合成斜体。
  • 使用全角标点(,。:;「」),标题结尾不加句号;中英文边界加盘古之白:「使用 Claude」「2024 年」。
