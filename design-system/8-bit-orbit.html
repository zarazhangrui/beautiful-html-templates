01 / Overview

A CRT, Rasterized

8-Bit Orbit is a retro-futuristic pixel-art system built on the 4-pixel unit: every shadow offset, border, and corner bracket resolves to a multiple of 4px. Atmospheric overlays — scanlines, grain, vignette glow, animated starfields — reinforce the illusion on every surface. The system reads as broken when surfaces are clean; always layer the atmosphere.

02 / Colours

Navy Ground, Three Neons

Deep navy as ground, lit by three saturated neons plus a soft lavender pastel. The neons never appear as body text — they are reserved for headlines, stat numerals, accent rules, and label fills, so they read as illuminated rather than printed.

03 / Typography

Display · Body · HUD

Three faces, single roles. Tektur for display, hero, and stat numerals; Chakra Petch for body; Space Mono for labels, badges, chart values, and counters — the HUD readout voice. Cross-mixing flattens the system.

05 / Depth & Shape

Hard Offset Shadows

Depth is stacked hard offset shadows in the pixel unit — zero blur, always stepping down-right in 4px increments. No corner is ever rounded; 0 4px 12px rgba(0,0,0,.1) does not exist here.

06 / Do & Don't

Keep It On-Grid

Do
  • Layer scanlines + grain + CRT-vignette on every surface — the atmosphere IS the system.
  • Snap every measurement to the 4px pixel unit.
  • Cyan headlines on dark; deep-navy headlines on colored grids.
  • Wrap every eyebrow in the navy label pill.
  • Give Tektur display its stacked text-shadow; stat numerals the 3px shadow.
  • Bracket cards with L-corners instead of full outlines.
Don't
  • Round any corner — square edges only.
  • Blur any shadow — every shadow is hard-edged.
  • Place neon text on a neon surface (illegible).
  • Introduce a fourth neon — cyan + pink + yellow + lavender only.
  • Substitute the fonts or cross their three roles.
  • Shadow with left or upward offsets — down-right only.
