Do & Don't

§ 07

Do

  • ★Apply the grain overlay to every slide — it's the surface everything prints on.
  • ★Use the stacked red text-shadow on all display type; scale the offset to the size.
  • ★Keep red exclusively as a shadow colour.
  • ★Use Caveat Brush only at 64px+, lowercase, slightly rotated, never alone.
  • ★Use 6px borders for major structural divisions.

Don't

  • ✕Don't use red as a background or text colour — it's shadow material.
  • ✕Don't set Alfa Slab One in mixed case — the uppercase lock is its authority.
  • ✕Don't round card corners or soften the 6px borders.
  • ✕Don't use Archivo Narrow for headlines.
  • ✕Don't vary the DM Mono size — 24px is the single fixed label size.

每段中文都要做的调整

  • 堆叠红影(橙→红→红深)完美转移:给每个中文展示标题用橙色 + 红影,活版凸印质感不变。
  • 毛笔插入声不转移 —— 中文书法读作庄重,留空或用一个拉丁 Caveat Brush 短词(如「yes!」)保留随性插入能量。
  • 放宽行高:正文 1.75–1.85,展示 1.15–1.25;字距归零;中文不做 uppercase。
  • 使用全角标点(,。:;「」),标题结尾不加句号;中英文边界加盘古之白:「使用 Claude」。
  • 菱形项目符、旋转印章、6px 边框、网点纸纹 —— 全部原样保留,与语言无关。
