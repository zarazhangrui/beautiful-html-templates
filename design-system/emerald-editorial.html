§ 01 — Overview01

Type leads,
not image.

The foundational premise is a single face — Bodoni Moda at weight 900 — used at unapologetic scale: 92px for routine headlines, up to 460px for hero numerals. The serif is loud, theatrical, confident. Every slide leads with type.

01
The section-opener panel

A solid navy panel holding a jumbo Bodoni numeral reads as a magazine title-page opening — heavy, anchored, ceremonial. The signature elevation device.

§ 06 — Depth & Elevation06

Inversion is
the depth.

01
Color-block inversion

A navy tile on the emerald canvas is the system's "elevated" element. Inversion replaces shadow entirely.

02
4px ink rules

Sections are separated by 4px navy lines that read as paper-fold creases between regions.

03
Solid panels

Strict rectangles with full inverse fill give tiles the weight of printed ink blocks. No glass, no neumorphism.

No box-shadow · no gradient · no blur06 / 10
§ 07 — Do & Don't07

Hold the
commitment.

Do

  • +Run every Bodoni headline at weight 900 with negative tracking and 0.9–0.95 leading.
  • +Run every Manrope label uppercase with at least 0.05em letter-spacing.
  • +Use 4px solid navy rules for every separator, list border, and tile divider.
  • +Reach for the double-rule ornament around small connector words.
  • +Keep every shape a strict rectangle — the no-radius commitment is the signature.

Don't

  • –Don't add box-shadow, gradient, or blur — the system is flat printed-ink.
  • –Don't run Bodoni at weight 500, or introduce a third typeface.
  • –Don't render Manrope in sentence case or without tracking.
  • –Don't introduce a fourth colour beyond emerald / navy / paper.
  • –Don't use border-radius, or 1–2px structural rules.
One display headline + 3–4 supporting tiles07 / 10

Universal CJK adjustments

  • 放宽行高 0.05–0.08:全宽方块比拉丁字母更重 —— 展示提到 1.0–1.1,正文提到 1.55–1.65。
  • 去掉中文标题负字距(Bodoni 展示用 -0.01 至 -0.03em 会挤压汉字),设为 letter-spacing 0。
  • 中文不做 text-transform: uppercase —— 拉丁 chrome 全大写的字段在中文里改用思源黑体的字重与字距承载。
  • 使用中文全角标点(,。:;「」),标题结尾不加句号;中英文边界加盘古之白:「2026 年 5 月」。
  • 展示用文楷暖意承载剧场感,巨号数字用宋体 900 取其分量;不要用黑体做展示,会拍平成「现代企业」。
